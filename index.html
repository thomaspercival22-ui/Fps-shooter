How to play

Touch

  • Left thumb anywhere on the left side: move. Push to the edge to sprint.
  • Right thumb drag: look around. Hold fire and slide your thumb to aim while shooting.
  • Aim button: aim down sights (sniper: scope).
  • Tap the weapon panel or the arrows to switch to your pistol.
  • Frag and Flashbang buttons throw grenades where you look.

Keyboard & mouse

  • WASD move, Shift sprint, Space jump, C crouch
  • Mouse look, left click fire, right click aim
  • R reload, Q / 1 / 2 switch, G frag, F flashbang, Esc pause

Tactics

  • Enemies work as a squad. They take cover, suppress you, flank, and throw grenades if you camp.
  • They hear gunfire, sprinting and reloading. When you reload close to them, they may rush you.
  • Flashbangs blind every enemy looking toward the blast for a few seconds. Push while they're stunned. Look away from your own flashbang.
  • Red dots on the minimap are enemies who just fired.
  • Bullets travel and drop. Wood and thin metal can be shot through. Concrete stops rounds.
  • Health regenerates after a few seconds out of fire. Dead enemies may drop ammo or medkits.
  • The ammo crate in the headquarters building refills ammo and grenades.
