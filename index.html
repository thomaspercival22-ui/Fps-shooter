How to play

Touch

  • Left thumb anywhere on the left side: move. Push to the edge to sprint.
  • Right thumb drag: look around. Hold fire and slide your thumb to aim while shooting.
  • Aim button: aim down sights (sniper: scope).
  • Tap the weapon panel or the arrows to switch to your pistol.
  • Frag and Flashbang buttons throw grenades where you look.
  • NVG flips down night vision, THRM cycles thermal (white-hot, black-hot, colour). FPV launches a kamikaze drone.

Keyboard & mouse

  • WASD move, Shift sprint, Space jump, C crouch
  • Mouse look, left click fire, right click aim
  • R reload (also clears stoppages), B fire selector, Q / 1 / 2 switch, G frag, F flashbang, Esc pause
  • N night vision, T thermal, V drone (Space / C for up / down, click to detonate, Q to exit)

Tactics

  • Enemies work as a squad. They take cover, suppress you, flank, and throw grenades if you camp.
  • They hear gunfire, sprinting and reloading. When you reload close to them, they may rush you.
  • Flashbangs blind every enemy looking toward the blast for a few seconds. Push while they're stunned. Look away from your own flashbang.
  • Red dots on the minimap are enemies who just fired.
  • Bullets travel and drop. Wood and thin metal can be shot through. Concrete stops rounds.
  • Magazines are tracked individually: a reload with rounds left keeps the partial mag, an empty reload drops it. The pips next to your ammo show full and partial mags.
  • Guns can jam (more often when the barrel is hot). Hit reload to tap-rack and clear it. Sustained fire heats the barrel: it smokes and the shots spread.
  • Health regenerates after a few seconds out of fire. Dead enemies may drop ammo or medkits.
  • The ammo crate in the headquarters building refills ammo, grenades and drones.
  • At night enemies struggle to see you, but their infrared lasers give them away under night vision. Thermal shows body heat through the dark.
  • The FPV drone's video gets worse with range and walls. Dive it into enemies or tap fire to detonate. Enemies will try to shoot it down.

Tower Hostage Rescue

  • No waves: clear floor 47 room by room. Hostage takers kill a hostage if you miss, wound them without dropping them, or let them see you coming. Flash the room, then take one clean shot.
  • Walk up to a hostage in a cleared room to cut them loose. Civilians are no-shoot targets.

Overwatch (sniper)

  • Crouch behind the sandbags to rest the rifle. LASE for the range, dial the ZERO to it, hold into the wind by the mil dots, and breathe out.
  • Your spotter calls every shot and marks the splash on your reticle with a dot. Put that dot on the target for the next shot. The marksman on the HQ roof is hunting you: if you are slow he kills your spotter, then you.
